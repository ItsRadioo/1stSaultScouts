Questions answered

Helpful information about joining, sections, uniforms, volunteering and the Trail Ready Program.

General questions

How do I join?

Use any Join Now button to open the MyScouts registration website, or contact the group if you are unsure which section is appropriate.

What section is right for my child?

Visit the Sections page for age ranges and program descriptions.

Can adults volunteer without previous Scouting experience?

Yes. Training and support are provided for approved volunteer roles.

Where can I find badge placement information?

Visit the Uniforms page and select the appropriate section.

What is the Trail Ready Program?

It is the group's proposed gear drive, lending and rental initiative intended to reduce equipment barriers for youth and families.
