Trail Ready Program

Helping every Scout
become trail ready

A gear drive, lending and rental initiative designed to make outdoor adventure more accessible for youth and families.

About the program

Reliable outdoor equipment should not be a barrier to participation

The Trail Ready Program is operated by the 1st Sault Ste. Marie Scout Group to help youth and families access appropriate outdoor equipment for meetings, camps, hikes and other Scouting adventures.

Through donated equipment, group-owned inventory and affordable lending or rental options, the program helps families participate without having to purchase every item immediately.

C

Confidence

Reliable equipment helps youth participate with confidence and focus on learning new skills.

A

Adventure

Access to proper gear opens the door to camps, hikes and new outdoor experiences.

Mentorship

Experienced volunteers help families select, use and care for outdoor equipment.

P

Preparedness

The right equipment supports safer, more comfortable and better prepared outdoor experiences.

How it works

Borrowing equipment

Final procedures, fees, deposits and lending periods will be approved before the program begins operating.

Review your needs

Identify the equipment required for the upcoming activity.

Contact the coordinator

Ask about availability, sizing and any applicable fees or deposits.

Reserve equipment

Available items are assigned for an approved lending period.

Pick up and inspect

Equipment condition and included components are reviewed together.

Return responsibly

Return all equipment clean, complete and by the agreed date.

Equipment donations

Give unused outdoor gear a new adventure

Families and community members may be able to donate clean, safe and serviceable outdoor equipment to support the Trail Ready Program.

  • Tents with all required components
  • Clean sleeping bags and pads
  • Backpacks in usable condition
  • Snowshoes and winter equipment
  • Lanterns and headlamps
  • Camp kitchen equipment

Donation acceptance

All donated equipment should be inspected before it is accepted into the program. Items may be declined because of age, condition, safety, cleanliness, missing components or storage limitations.

Fuel, food, damaged safety equipment and items that cannot be cleaned or safely tested should not be accepted.
